# Contributing

Bug reports and questions are welcome as [issues](https://github.com/caldrix-ai/aerix/issues/new/choose).

This repository is generated from aerix's private source, and every change made here is overwritten by the next update, so pull requests are not accepted. If you found a mistake in the docs, open an issue and we will fix it at the source.

A useful bug report has:

- the output of `aerix --version`;
- the output of `caldrix --version`;
- your `pool.members` entry from `~/.config/aerix/settings.json`, or "none";
- the exact command you ran;
- what you expected, and what happened instead, with the full output.

Do not paste session logs: they hold your code and prompts.
